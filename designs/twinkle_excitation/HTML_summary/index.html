Optiland vs Zemax

TWINKLE excitation path: Optiland against Zemax

The full excitation path of the TWINKLE two-photon microscope in Optiland: a 4.39 mm beam, galvo mirror, relay, scan lens, tube lens, three fold mirrors, a tilted dichroic and an ideal objective. The Zemax file stores 22 galvo angles. Optiland loads only the first until we rewrite the file, and once we do, ray tracing agrees with Zemax to 0.14% on every surface Zemax computed a semi-diameter for. The PSF-based results Zemax gives (FWHM, field curvature) cannot be computed in Optiland at all, because its paraxial engine rejects the tilted galvo.

optiland 0.6.3 λ 0.92 µm Zemax OpticStudio 22.2.1 EPD 4.39 mm fields 0° run 2026-11-25
Not working on this page
  • Wavefront, PSF, Strehl and FWHM. Optiland refuses to compute them on tilted or multi-fold systems, so Zemax's PSF plots cannot be compared. Cause: Optiland's paraxial engine (issue 8; upstream roadmap #865). Upstream: #865 (roadmap, with our comment), #1210.
  • Optiland's 3D viewer. Shows only the first leg of the path and misplaces elements. Cause: not located (issue 10). Upstream: not yet filed.
  • Galvo angles and tilted surfaces on import. Only configuration 1 loads and the tilted dichroic is ignored unless our workaround rewrites the file first. Anything loading these files without the workaround is silently wrong. Cause: Optiland's Zemax reader silently drops them (issues 5 to 7). Upstream: #1247.

Zemax angle scan: PSF width and focus shift

Blocked in Optiland

The TWINKLE authors scanned the galvo from −10° to +10° in 0.5° steps and, at each angle, a Huygens PSF through focus in 0.1 µm steps (MATLAB driving the Zemax API; script in Zemax_reference_output/). They squared the PSF for two-photon excitation and measured radial FWHM, axial FWHM and the focus position. Values below are read from the vector markers of the MATLAB plot (digitised, not Zemax numeric output).

On axis the two-photon radial FWHM is 0.77 µm and the axial FWHM 5.1 µm; both grow to 1.35 and 7.8 µm at ±10°. Focus moves by about ±0.4 µm.

Optiland cannot reproduce this

FFT PSF, wavefront and Strehl all raise UnsupportedParaxialGeometryError on this file (issue 8). There is no number to compare. Real rays do give a geometric picture below: the RMS spot radius is 0.05 to 0.33 µm, inside the 0.90 µm Airy radius at every angle, so the design is diffraction-limited on this evidence. That is a prediction to check against the Zemax widths once a PSF can be computed, not a match.

Spots and scan linearity

Works

At each of the 20 distinct galvo angles we traced a 24-ring hexapolar pupil to the image plane (Zemax surface 39, after the ideal objective). The chief ray moves -45.7 µm per degree of galvo angle, in a straight line to within a few µm, and the RMS spot radius stays below 0.35 µm. Dashed: the Airy radius from the real marginal-ray NA of 0.63.

Optiland issues found

Full details are in optiland_issues_found.md (issues 5 to 10 are new with these designs).

  • 5. Surface tilts (SCBD) ignored. The tilted dichroic loads untilted, with no warning. Patched locally Upstream: #1247.
  • 6. Parameter pickups (PPAR) ignored. Only matters when a value is edited. Patched locally Upstream: #1247.
  • 7. Multi-configuration data dropped. Only configuration 1 loads; the galvo angles are configurations. Worked around Upstream: #1247.
  • 8. Paraxial engine refuses tilted systems. Blocks wavefront, PSF and Strehl on the tilted galvo systems. Open Upstream: #865 (roadmap, with our comment), #1210.
  • 9. 2D layout mangles folded systems. This page draws its own projection. Worked around Upstream: not yet filed.
  • 10. 3D viewer shows one leg of the excitation path. Cause not located. Open Upstream: not yet filed.
